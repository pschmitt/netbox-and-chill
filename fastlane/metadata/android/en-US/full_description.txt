NetBox and Chill is a native Android companion app for your self-hosted NetBox instance.

Point the camera at the QR/barcode sticker on a physical device (the same sticker that links to
its public NetBox URL, e.g. https://netbox.example.com/dcim/devices/393/) and jump straight to
that device's details - status, site, rack, role, manufacturer/model, serial, asset tag, and
primary IP.

The device inventory is cached locally, so browsing and searching your devices works even
without connectivity in the server room. Cached data syncs automatically in the background and on
pull-to-refresh.

To use this app you need your own NetBox instance and an API token.

Built with Material 3, distributed via Obtainium/GitHub releases only - not on any app store.
